## The learnings

Of course I couldn't pass up this opportunity to recreate it. It looks strangely similar to the layout used by pikchr!

The key learnings for me was to use lines/splines to create the "speedometer" semi-circle. If you count the segments in the original, there's 11 every 90 degrees.

The other big learning was the use of macros. They shrunk the script big time. They made the speedometer much more readable and the triangles as well.

```pikchr {id="dissecting-the-story-of-mel"}
$circleStepSize = .15
$circleStep = 90/11

define circleSplice {
 line from previous line.end go $circleStepSize heading $circleStep*$1
 spline from 1/2 between previous line.start and previous line.end then go .1 heading $circleStep*$1 + 90
}

line invisible
circleSplice(0)
circleSplice(1)
circleSplice(2)
circleSplice(3)
circleSplice(4)
circleSplice(5)
circleSplice(6)
circleSplice(7)
circleSplice(8)
circleSplice(9)
circleSplice(10)
circleSplice(11)
circleSplice(12)
circleSplice(13)
circleSplice(14)
circleSplice(15)
circleSplice(16)
circleSplice(17)
circleSplice(18)
circleSplice(19)
circleSplice(20)
circleSplice(21)
circleSplice(22)

define triangle {
  $1: line from ($2) \
    go .6cm heading (150 + $3) \
    then .6cm heading (-90 + $3) \
    then .6cm heading (30 + $3) \
    close
}

RHeadPoint: dot at 12th line.end + (0cm, 1mm) invisible
triangle(RHead, RHeadPoint, 180)
RHeadBack: dot at 1/2 way between 2nd vertex of RHead and 3rd vertex of RHead invisible

RWHeadPoint: dot at 16th line.end + (.5mm, 1mm) invisible
triangle(RWHead, RWHeadPoint, $circleStep * 14 + 90)
RWHeadBack: dot at 1/2 way between 2nd vertex of RWHead and 3rd vertex of RWHead invisible

WHeadPoint: dot at 20th line.end + (1mm, .5mm) invisible
triangle(WHead, WHeadPoint, $circleStep * 18 + 90)
WHeadBack: dot at 1/2 way between 2nd vertex of WHead and 3rd vertex of WHead invisible

text at 2nd vertex of RHead + (-2mm, 0mm) "READ HEAD" big rjust
text at RWHeadBack + (.5cm, .5cm) "READ/WRITE HEAD" ljust big "TRACK 127" ljust big
line from RWHeadBack go up .7cm then go right .4cm
text at 3rd vertex of WHead + (2mm, 0mm) "WRITE HEAD" big ljust

$corner = .25

Rise: line from RHeadBack up .5cm
TopLeftArc: arc cw from Rise.end to Rise.end + ($corner, $corner)

TopRun: line from TopLeftArc.end right until even with last text.e + ($corner/2, 0)
TopRightArc: arc cw from TopRun.end to TopRun.end + ($corner, -$corner)

Drop: line from TopRightArc.end down until even with WHeadBack + (0, $corner)
BottomArc: arc cw from Drop.end to Drop.end + (-$corner, -$corner)
line from BottomArc.end left until even with WHeadBack
```
